# The project's view: this week and its pages — 2026-11-21

Looking at a real thesis project in the sidebar, thirteen day rows ("BY
DAY", every day the project ever had) took two thirds of it, while the two
pages that gather them, the project's page (P) and the week's (W), were two
grey caption lines under the name, read as metadata rather than as places.

The mockups were drawn from the student's own notes, so they are not kept
here. What they compared:

- **Now · A · B.** A: "By day" cut to this week (Monday to Sunday, as the
  week's page counts it), the days before folded to one line that names
  their meetings. B: A, with one row per paper instead of one per day.
- **Three placements of the pages, on A.** A1: two boxes side by side under
  the name. A2: each where its subject is — the project's page under the
  name, the week's heading "This week", so the rows under it read as its
  preview. A3: a "Pages" section of two plain rows.
- **Four ways to tell a paper from a note.** A word (PAPER, NOTE); a thin
  rule and italics (a quote, a cited work); italics only; a glyph (❝ for
  someone else's words, ✎ for the person's own writing).

Chosen: A3's glyphs and Earlier fold, with A2's two boxes where A2 put them.
`built.png`: the page as built (test vault, made up), as it opens, with the
week's page picked, and with Earlier opened (`tests/page/shots.py --scene
project`). The design is recorded in `docs/plan.md`, "This week and its
pages".
