# Longhand identity exploration — round one

Historical studies from 17 October 2026, all superseded or not chosen.
Use the canonical assets in [the logo source of truth](../../README.md).

## Brief

Longhand should feel like a maker of writing tools. Explore a minimal abstract
emblem with five lobes or shoulders, drawing on the restraint and repeated
presence of a writing-instrument maker's signature. Give Longhand its own
silhouette. Use one colour, robust small-size rendering, and consistent geometry
wherever the emblem appears. Do not assign invented meanings to the five parts.

## Studies

- **A / The seal:** five equal rounded lobes; the closest to the initial idea.
- **B / The shoulder:** a rounded pentagon, with no inward scallops.
- **C / The impression:** five lobes with unequal pressure and a slight lean.

Each has a secondary contour to explore degree of softness. `comparison.png`
shows the principal marks, alternates, actual 16/24/32 px sizes, reversed marks,
and hypothetical launcher/page placements. The context sketches explore repeat
use; they are not proposed changes to the product's UI or typography.

Standalone SVG marks use `currentColor`. Rebuild the comparison with
`python3 docs/brand/explorations/round-one/draw.py` (requires `rsvg-convert`).

These are original geometric sketches, not a uniqueness or trademark clearance.
