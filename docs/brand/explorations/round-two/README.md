# Longhand identity exploration — round two

Historical studies from 17 October 2026. **Pedro chose A2**; A1, C1, and C2
were not chosen. A2 is the approved ancestor of the active Open cuts refinement.
Use the canonical assets in [the logo source of truth](../../README.md), rather
than an SVG from this historical folder.

Feedback on round one: the rounded pentagon was solid but unremarkable; the
seal felt too much like a star; the asymmetric impression felt too much like a
blob. Continue the seal and impression families.

This round widens the outer crowns and concentrates the indentations into
shorter valleys. It bounds asymmetric adjustments tightly rather than using
free-form deformation. A1 and A2 develop the regular seal, C1 and C2 develop
the impression. C2 also tests a slightly squared body.

All four contours have five recesses. Small-size studies, reversal, and repeated
launcher/wordmark use are shown in `comparison.png`. Typeface is contextual,
not a proposed brand decision. Standalone SVGs use `currentColor`.

Rebuild with `python3 docs/brand/explorations/round-two/draw.py` (rsvg-convert).
